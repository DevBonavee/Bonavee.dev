## The problem

Organisers collected sign-ups in group chats and spreadsheets, so attendance numbers were guesswork.

## What I built

- Event pages with one-tap sign-up and calendar invites
- Automatic reminders the day before each event
- QR check-in at the door with live attendance counts

## Results

Three communities now run their events on it, and check-in at the door takes less than half the time it used to.
