## The problem

Staff spent hours digging through policy PDFs, and answers in the team chat were often outdated or wrong.

## What I built

- Upload and indexing pipeline with chunking and embeddings
- Retrieval with page-level citations shown next to every answer
- Streaming chat UI with feedback buttons to flag weak answers

## How it works

Each PDF is split into overlapping chunks, embedded and stored in Postgres with pgvector. A question pulls the closest chunks, the model answers only from them, and the UI shows the page each claim came from.

## Results

Staff stopped asking the same policy questions in chat, and every answer now points to the page it came from.
