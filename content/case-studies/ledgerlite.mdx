## The problem

Traders tracked sales in notebooks, so closing the day took 30+ minutes and nobody knew their real profit at month end.

## What I built

- Mobile-first sales and expense entry that works on slow connections
- A FastAPI backend with typed SQLModel models and Alembic migrations
- A nightly job that texts each trader their totals and best-selling items

## How it works

Sales are saved on the phone first and sync when the connection comes back. Each night a scheduled job adds up the day per trader and sends one SMS with totals and the three best sellers.

## Results

Closing the day went from half an hour of adding up a notebook to a glance at one text message, and more than 120 traders signed up in the first two months.
